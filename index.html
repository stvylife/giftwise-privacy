GiftWise — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026 · App: GiftWise (com.stvylife.giftwise) · Developer: Stevenson Nwokenkwo (StvyLiFE Productions)

GiftWise is a gift-planning and budgeting app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: your data stays on your device, and we do not collect, sell, or share it.

Information we collect

None. GiftWise has no user accounts and no servers operated by us. Everything you enter — the people you add, birthdays, interests, wishlists, gift plans, budgets, and settings — is stored locally on your device. We never receive, see, or store it.

Optional AI gift suggestions

GiftWise includes an optional feature, turned off by default, that generates gift ideas using Google’s Gemini API. If you choose to enable it, you provide your own Google Gemini API key, and the details needed to make a suggestion (such as a person’s name, relationship, interests, and budget) are sent from your device directly to Google under your own key, over an encrypted connection, only at the moment you request a suggestion. We do not receive or retain this information. Your use of the Gemini API is governed by Google’s API terms and Google’s Privacy Policy. If you never enable this feature, no data ever leaves your device.

Data sharing

We do not share your data with anyone. There are no advertisers, no analytics providers, and no third-party trackers in GiftWise.

Data storage, security & retention

Your data is held in your device’s private app storage and remains there until you delete it. You can export your data yourself (as a file you choose to share) from within the app. Because the data lives only on your device, its security is tied to your device’s own protections.

Deleting your data

You are in full control. Clear the app’s data (Android Settings → Apps → GiftWise → Storage → Clear data) or uninstall the app to permanently remove everything. There is nothing stored on our side to delete.

Children’s privacy

GiftWise is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
